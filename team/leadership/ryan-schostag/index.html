Executive Overview

Ryan Schostag is CEO and Chief Technology Officer at Igzibo, leading the company's strategic vision, technology direction, and engineering execution. With 10+ years of hands-on experience as a Senior Data Engineer, Python Backend Developer, and DevOps Engineer, Ryan brings both strategic leadership and deep technical expertise to guide the organization.

Throughout his career, Ryan has architected and delivered mission-critical systems at leading organizations including Motion/Wells Fargo, DFIN, Fisker, and Capital One. He specializes in designing enterprise data platforms, modernizing ETL/ELT pipelines, building high-performance microservices, and implementing cloud-native solutions at scale. His technical foundation in Python, SQL, cloud architecture (Azure/AWS), data engineering, and DevOps practices directly informs his strategic leadership decisions.
